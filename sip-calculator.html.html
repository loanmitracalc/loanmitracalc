SIP Calculator

Our SIP Calculator helps you estimate the potential value of your monthly Systematic Investment Plan investment. Enter your monthly investment, expected annual return and investment period to estimate your total investment and potential returns.

How Does a SIP Calculator Work?

A SIP calculator estimates the future value of regular investments using the monthly investment amount, expected rate of return and investment duration. The actual returns from mutual funds or other investments may vary depending on market performance.

SIP Calculator Formula

The estimated future value of a SIP is calculated using the monthly investment, expected monthly rate of return and total number of months.

Example

For example, if you invest ₹5,000 every month for 10 years and assume an expected annual return of 12%, the calculator can estimate your total investment and potential future value.

Important Note

SIP calculator results are estimates for informational purposes only. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks, and actual returns may be different from the estimated results.
