Personal Loan EMI Calculator

Our Personal Loan EMI Calculator helps you estimate your loan repayment, interest and EMI based on the loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure. You can select interest rate per year, month or day and choose loan tenure in years, months or days.

Interest Calculation

The calculator shows the estimated total interest for the selected loan period. It also shows the equivalent average interest per year, month and day to make the result easier to understand.

Loan Tenure Options

You can calculate the loan tenure in years, months or days. When days are selected, the calculator displays an estimated Daily EMI. When years or months are selected, it displays an estimated Monthly EMI.

Important Note

The results shown by this calculator are estimates for informational purposes only. Actual loan interest, EMI, processing fees, charges and repayment terms may vary depending on the lender and loan agreement. Year, month and day conversions use standard approximate periods.
