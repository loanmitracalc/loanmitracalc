How to Calculate Personal Loan EMI?

Personal Loan EMI depends on the loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure. A longer tenure can reduce the monthly EMI, but it may increase the total interest paid over the loan period.

Personal Loan EMI Formula

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)N ÷ ((1 + R)N − 1)

Here, P is the principal loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate and N is the total number of monthly instalments.
